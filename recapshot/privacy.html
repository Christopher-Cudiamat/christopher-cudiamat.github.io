Recapshot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Recapshot ("the app") is a screenshot companion that helps you come back to the screenshots you'd otherwise forget. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

In short: everything stays on your phone. Recapshot has no account and no server, and it does not upload your screenshots, notes, voice notes or anything it reads from your screenshots. It does not use your location.

What the app accesses, and why

Photos: your screenshots

Why
To show them, sort them by app and category, read the text in them (on-device), find duplicates, and remind you about them.

Where it goes
Stays on your phone. The app only reads screenshot albums. It changes your gallery only when you ask: deleting a screenshot, or moving one into the vault (below). Your phone confirms each time.

Text read from screenshots

Why
On-device text recognition (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iOS) powers search, sorting, quick actions (copy a code, call a number, add a date), and cleanup suggestions.

Where it goes
Stored in a local database on your phone. You can delete it any time (Me → Smart sorting → off → Delete the text it read).

Notes, links, to-dos, reminders, edits

Why
Things you add to a screenshot.

Where it goes
Stored locally on your phone.

Microphone (optional)

Why
Only when you tap "Record a voice note".

Where it goes
The recording is saved in the app's private folder on your phone.

Notifications (optional)

Why
Reminders, the daily recap, "Why did you save this?" prompts.

Where it goes
Local notifications scheduled on your phone.

Clipboard

Why
Only read when you tap Paste (to attach a copied link). The app may check whether the clipboard holds text, without reading it, to decide whether to show the Paste button.

Where it goes
Not stored except as the link you chose to attach.

Vault (optional)

Why
Screenshots you move into the vault leave your gallery and are kept in the app's private folder, behind a vault PIN and your fingerprint or face. Moving one out puts it back in your gallery.

Where it goes
The vault holds the only copy, so uninstalling the app deletes vaulted screenshots. Move them out first. The vault PIN is stored only as a salted hash.

Fingerprint / face

Why
To open the vault, and to confirm it's you if you forget the vault PIN. Uses your phone's own check.

Where it goes
The app never sees your biometric data.

Network use

Recapshot has no server of its own. Your screenshots and everything about them stay on your phone. Only two things use the internet:

  • Google Play checks whether you bought Pro.
  • Google ML Kit usage statistics. Recapshot reads text and finds faces with Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. ML Kit sends Google anonymous usage statistics to keep it working well: your device model and Android version, the app's name and version, a random per-install ID that doesn't identify you or your phone, and how the reading performed (speed, sizes, errors). Google uses this for diagnostics and analytics, sends it over HTTPS, and doesn't pass it to anyone else. It never includes your screenshots, the text read from them, your notes or the faces themselves.

Links you open (source links, links found in screenshots) open in your browser or the relevant app.

Purchases

Recapshot Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. It only learns from Google Play whether Pro is unlocked on your account.

Sharing

When you share a screenshot, you choose where it goes. If "Add source when sharing" is on, the shared image includes a strip with your note, the source link and "via Recapshot". You can turn this off in Me.

Backups are files you create and save wherever you choose. The app doesn't upload them.

Data retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored: notes, read text, voice notes, settings and screenshots in the vault. Screenshots in your gallery stay there.
  • Deleting a screenshot in the app also deletes its notes, reminders, voice note and read text.

Children

Recapshot is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
