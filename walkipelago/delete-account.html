Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Walkipelago and go to Me.
  2. Scroll to Settings → Account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type your nickname to confirm.

Your account is deleted at once.

Without the app

Email [contact address to come] from the Google email you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

Everything we keep: your step totals, the towns you visited, your streak, badges, nickname, avatar, place on Ranks, shop items and your sign-in. It drops out of backup copies within 7 days. Steps in Health Connect stay on your phone; they belong to Health Connect.

Google Play keeps its own record of any purchase, as it does for every app.
