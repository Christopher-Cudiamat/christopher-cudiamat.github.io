Walkipelago

Privacy policy

Updated 24 October 2026

Walkipelago is a walking game: your real steps let you visit real towns across the Philippines. This page says what we keep, why, and what you can do about it. The same words are in the app under Me → Settings → Privacy.

What we keep

Nothing, until you sign in. Before that, everything stays on your phone.

After you sign in with Google, we keep:

StepsOne total a day
Busiest hourOne step count a day (no time, no place)
Towns you visitWhich town, which day
Town check-insYes or no
Nickname, flag, hometown and avatarShown on Ranks
Your Google email and account idOnly to sign you in

We never keep step-by-step records, your location, photos, contacts, other health data, or your Google name and picture. From Health Connect the app reads only your step count, and those records stay on your phone.

Why

To keep your towns safe on a new phone, to put you on the Ranks boards, and to check that steps are real so the boards stay fair. Nothing is sold.

Who sees it

Other players see your nickname, flag, hometown, avatar, pinned badges, steps and towns on Ranks and on friends' boards. Nobody sees your email.

Where it is kept

On Supabase servers in Singapore. Google handles sign-in, and Google Play handles purchases.

How long

As long as you have an account. You can delete it any time in the app: Me → Settings → Delete my account. Everything we keep is deleted at once, and drops out of backup copies within 7 days. Without the app, see Delete your account.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you can ask what we keep, get a copy, have it corrected, or have it deleted. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Ages

Walkipelago is for ages 13 and up. The app asks your birth year once and keeps only "13 or older: yes", on your phone.
